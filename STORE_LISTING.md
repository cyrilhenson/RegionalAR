## App Name
RegionalAR

## Short Description (156 characters)
Explore 3D medical imaging in AR. View CT and MRI scans with interactive tissue layers, cut planes, and anatomical annotations for Regional Anesthesia training.

## Long Description (1,497 characters)

RegionalAR brings medical imaging into augmented reality on Meta Quest. Designed for clinicians and educators in Regional Anesthesia, it lets you interact with real DICOM CT and MRI scans as 3D holographic volumes in your physical space.

Grab, rotate, and scale volumes with Quest controllers. Toggle independent tissue layers — bone, vasculature, nerves, and muscle — each rendered as a high-quality mesh using AI segmentation. Slice through anatomy with dual adjustable cut planes, fine-tune opacity to reveal deep structures, and place color-coded markers to annotate landmarks or injection sites during teaching.

Process your own DICOM scans on desktop with the companion RegionalAR Desktop app, then wirelessly transfer them to your Quest over local WiFi. Auto-discovery means no IP entry needed. A Head and Neck CTA sample volume is included so you can start exploring immediately.

RegionalAR is an educational and training tool. It is not intended for clinical diagnosis or patient care.

## Release Notes — v1.0.0 (Alpha)

Initial alpha release.

- Volumetric rendering of CT and MRI DICOM scans in AR
- AI-segmented tissue layers: bone, vasculature, nerves, muscle
- Dual interactive cut planes with cross-sectional view
- Grab, rotate, and scale volumes with Quest controllers
- Floating control panel with tissue toggles, opacity slider, and markers
- Wireless scan transfer from desktop companion app via local WiFi
- Scan library for managing multiple volumes on-device
- Bundled Head & Neck CTA sample volume
- Audio and haptic feedback on all interactions
